# 📊 HR Analytics & Employee Attrition Dashboard (Tableau)

> An end-to-end interactive Tableau dashboard built to evaluate workforce demographics, department-wise employee attrition rates, and key retention metrics for strategic HR decision-making.

---

## 📌 Project Overview
Workforce retention and attrition management are critical for maintaining organizational stability and productivity. This Tableau dashboard was designed to provide human resources stakeholders with deep, actionable insights into headcounts, active employee distributions, attrition trends, and high-risk employee segments across departments.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools
* **BI & Visualization:** Tableau Desktop / Tableau Public
* **Data Processing & Prep:** Microsoft Excel (Flat File Storage)
* **Design Architecture:** Custom background and theme conceptualized in PowerPoint; optimized for clear visual hierarchy and corporate presentation standards.

---

## 📂 Data Source & Schema
* **Dataset:** Employee records dataset containing approximately **1,470 rows** and **39 columns** capturing biographical metrics, job roles, satisfaction ratings, and active/attrition statuses.
* **Key Fields Used:** Employee Count, Attrition Status (Yes/No), Department, Education Field, Age, Job Role, and Job Satisfaction Ratings.

---

## 🔑 Key Features & Highlights

### 🎯 Business Problem & Objectives
* **Problem Statement:** HR executives lacked consolidated visibility into why high-performing or young talent was leaving the organization and which departments suffered the highest attrition turnover.
* **Dashboard Goal:** Provide an interactive, multi-view analytical system to track real-time headcount shifts, department-wise losses, and employee satisfaction trends.

### 📊 Visualizations & Chart Architecture
1. **Executive KPI Cards:** High-visibility banner metrics displaying **Total Employees**, **Active Employees**, **Attrition Count**, **Attrition Rate (%)**, and **Average Employee Age**.
2. **Gender Attrition Lollipop Chart:** Custom lollipop chart visualizing turnover distribution across genders.
3. **Department-Wise Attrition Pie Chart:** Percentage-based breakdown illustrating which business units (e.g., Sales vs. R&D) experience the heaviest turnover.
4. **Employee Age Frequency Chart (Custom Bins):** Dynamic parameter-driven frequency chart evaluating headcounts across adjustable age brackets.
5. **Job Satisfaction Matrix (Heatmap):** Detailed cross-tabulation mapping job roles against 1-to-4 satisfaction ratings to highlight engagement bottlenecks.
6. **Education Field Analysis:** Horizontal bar chart comparing attrition volumes across different academic backgrounds.
7. **Age & Gender Donut Charts:** Multi-view breakdown analyzing vulnerable age cohorts.

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights & Findings
* **Departmental Risk:** The **Sales** department accounted for the highest attrition proportion (>55% of total departures), signaling a need for targeted compensation or work-life balance reviews.
* **Age Vulnerability:** Peak attrition occurs heavily among younger professionals aged **25–34**, indicating potential flight risks regarding early career growth and progression paths.
* **Engagement Drivers:** Lower job satisfaction ratings in specific operational roles directly correlated with higher individual turnover rates.
